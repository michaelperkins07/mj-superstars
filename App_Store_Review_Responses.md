## RESPONSE GUIDELINES

1. **Respond within 24 hours** — especially 1-2 star reviews
2. **Never be defensive** — always empathetic and solution-oriented
3. **Always include support email** for negative reviews (support@topperformer.ai)
4. **Personalize when possible** — reference something specific from their review
5. **Keep it concise** — 2-4 sentences max
6. **Never offer refunds publicly** — handle via email
7. **Never argue** — a bad public response is worse than a bad review
8. **Thank everyone** — even the harsh critics took time to write
